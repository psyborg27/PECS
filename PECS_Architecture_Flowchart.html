PECS-PRO vs PECS-LITE

flowchart LR A[PECS Shared Principles] A --> B[Continuity Reconstruction] A --> C[Canonical Locality] A --> D[Duplicate Awareness] A --> E[Entropy Reduction] B --> F[PECS-PRO] C --> F D --> F E --> F B --> G[PECS-LITE] C --> G D --> G E --> G F --> H[Large Projects] F --> I[Long Runtime Sessions] F --> J[Incremental Updates] F --> K[Continuity Archaeology] G --> L[Local Models] G --> M[Small Contexts] G --> N[Compact Retrieval] G --> O[Lightweight Runtime]
Both systems share the same continuity philosophy but target different runtime complexity levels.
  • PECS-PRO: larger workspaces, richer continuity reconstruction, incremental persistence
  • PECS-LITE: constrained inference, compact continuity routing, low overhead
  • Both: deterministic continuity infrastructure, not autonomous engineering intelligence
